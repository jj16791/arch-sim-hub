The State of Open-Source Computer Architecture Simulators

Shared problems and community action. A working session bringing together users and developers across the whole open-source simulation community to turn shared problems into community-owned next steps.

SC26 · Chicago November 2026 60-minute BoF Ref. BoF197s1 Day & time TBC

About this session

This BoF builds on our SCA/HPCAsia26 session, where we mapped the shared problems facing open-source computer architecture simulators. This time the aim is to move from naming those problems to acting on them.

Whole-community by design

The discussion intends to serve the entire open-source architecture-simulation community, that includes both users and developers of many kinds of tools (cycle-level, functional, network/PDES, emulation, ML-driven, and more), rather than any single niche.

Action, not just discussion

Potential discussion topics are framed to lead towards something the community can collectively do, and everything we agree is captured here so the outcomes reach people who couldn't be in the room.

Format

An interactive, discussion-led session: short panellist presentations, then most of the hour for the room to talk, prioritise, and identify actionable ways forward.

  • 0–5 min Welcome & framing — the goal, and a recap of what SCA/HPCAsia26 surfaced.
  • 5–23 min Panellist presentations — short introductions and perspectives.
  • 23–25 min Setting the agenda — review of primary discussion topics from pre-generated and audience-steered themes.
  • 25–50 min Moderated discussion — working through the themes, with live audience input.
  • 50–60 min Converge — agree the priorities and next steps, captured live into this hub.

Outcomes

This hub is where the session's outputs live, so the discussion and takeaways reach the wider community, not just the people in the room.

  • Prioritised problems & next steps — posted here after the session.
  • Slides & materials — links to panellist framings and resources.
  • SCA/HPCAsia26 summary — a write-up of the previous iteration's discussion and takeaways.
